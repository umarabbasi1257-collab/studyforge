Calculate your exact age in years, months and days. You can also see your total days and weeks, plus the time remaining until your next birthday.

Your exact age
Enter your dates above and click Calculate Exact Age.

How the age calculator works

The calculator compares your date of birth with the selected calculation date and reports completed years, remaining months and remaining days using calendar dates.

What the result includes

Exact age = completed years + remaining months + remaining days Total days = calendar days between the two dates Total weeks = total days ÷ 7 Next birthday = the next occurrence of your birth month and day after the calculation date.
Calendar calculations account for leap years and different month lengths. This page uses calendar dates rather than assuming every year has exactly 365 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth and the date on which you want to calculate your age, then select Calculate Exact Age.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The calculation uses calendar dates and accounts for leap years and different month lengths.

Can I calculate my age on a future date?

Yes. Enter the future date in the calculation-date field. The date must be the same as or later than your date of birth.

How many days old am I?

Enter your date of birth and today's date, then the result will include the total number of calendar days between those dates.
