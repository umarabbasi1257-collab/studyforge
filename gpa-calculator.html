GPA Calculator

Calculate a weighted semester GPA by entering each course, its grade and credit hours. StudyForge uses the standard weighted-GPA formula and shows the calculation clearly.

How GPA is calculated

A weighted GPA is calculated by multiplying each course's grade-point value by its credit hours, adding those quality points, and dividing by the total credit hours.

GPA = Σ(grade points × credit hours) ÷ Σ(credit hours) Example: Course A: 4.0 grade points × 3 credits = 12 quality points Course B: 3.0 grade points × 3 credits = 9 quality points Total quality points = 21 Total credits = 6 GPA = 21 ÷ 6 = 3.50

Grade-point scale used by this calculator

A / A+ = 4.0 A− = 3.7 B+ = 3.3 B = 3.0 B− = 2.7 C+ = 2.3 C = 2.0 C− = 1.7 D+ = 1.3 D = 1.0 F = 0.0 Note: GPA scales vary between schools and universities. If your institution uses a different scale, use the grade-point values specified by your institution.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a weighted GPA?

A weighted GPA accounts for the number of credit hours assigned to each course, so courses with more credits have a larger effect on the result.

How many courses can I add?

You can add as many course rows as needed for the calculation. Courses with zero or blank credits are ignored.

Is this GPA scale universal?

No. Universities and schools can use different grading scales. This calculator uses a common 4.0-style scale and lets you enter the grade-point values that best match your institution.
