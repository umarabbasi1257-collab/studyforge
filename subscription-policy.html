Subscription & Refund Policy

StudyForge Pro is a recurring monthly subscription. The following rules explain renewal, cancellation and refunds.

Monthly renewal

StudyForge Pro renews automatically each billing period unless the customer cancels before the next renewal. If the customer does not cancel, the subscription continues and the next billing charge is processed normally.

Cancellation

Customers can cancel their subscription to stop future renewals. Cancellation does not permanently prevent the customer from subscribing again later.

Refunds

Refunds are not issued automatically when a subscription is cancelled. Refund decisions are handled manually by StudyForge. A full or partial refund may be issued when appropriate, subject to the payment provider's rules and applicable law.

Re-subscribing

After cancellation, a customer may purchase StudyForge Pro again when they want continued premium access.

Billing issues

Duplicate charges, billing errors and other payment problems can be reviewed manually. Customers should contact StudyForge with the relevant transaction details.
