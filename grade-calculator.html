How grade averaging works

Enter the percentage grade for each subject. The calculator finds the simple arithmetic average when all subjects have equal weight.

If your subjects have different credit weights, use a GPA or weighted-grade method instead of a simple average.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add more subjects?

Yes. Select Add Subject to create as many subject rows as you need.

Is this a weighted GPA calculator?

No. This page calculates a simple percentage average and gives a basic letter-grade estimate.
