What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the regular amount paid each month toward a loan and normally includes both principal and interest.

EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1) P = principal loan amount r = monthly interest rate as a decimal n = total number of monthly payments Monthly rate = annual interest rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100 Total repayment = EMI × number of payments Total interest = total repayment − principal

Example

Suppose you borrow $500,000 at 10% annual interest for 5 years. Loan amount = $500,000 Annual rate = 10% Tenure = 5 × 12 = 60 monthly payments The calculator determines the monthly EMI, total repayment and estimated total interest.
This calculator provides an estimate. Actual payments can differ because of lender-specific rates, fees, taxes, insurance, rounding, changing rates or other loan terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is EMI calculated?

EMI uses the loan principal, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly payments.

Does a higher interest rate increase EMI?

Yes. With the same loan amount and tenure, a higher rate generally increases monthly EMI and total interest.

Does a longer tenure reduce EMI?

Usually yes, but a longer tenure can increase total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Can I use this for a home, car or personal loan?

Yes. It can estimate payments for many fixed-rate installment loans.
