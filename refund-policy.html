Refund Policy

Information about StudyForge Pro subscriptions, cancellations and refund requests.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. StudyForge Pro Subscription

StudyForge Pro is an optional monthly subscription that provides access to premium StudyForge features and educational tools.

The current advertised subscription price is $4.99 USD per month.

2. Automatic Renewal

StudyForge Pro subscriptions renew automatically at the applicable monthly billing interval unless the customer cancels the subscription before the next renewal.

If a customer does not cancel the subscription, the subscription continues and the applicable recurring charge may be collected.

3. Cancellation

Customers may cancel their StudyForge Pro subscription at any time.

Cancellation prevents future recurring renewals. Cancelling a subscription does not automatically generate a refund for the current billing period.

4. Refunds

Refunds are not automatically issued when a subscription is cancelled.

Refund requests are reviewed manually by StudyForge. Depending on the circumstances, StudyForge may approve a full refund, partial refund, or decline a refund request.

Factors that may be considered include the date of purchase, subscription usage, duplicate charges, billing errors and the circumstances of the request.

5. Partial Refunds

Where appropriate, StudyForge may provide a partial refund for unused subscription time. Any such refund is discretionary and is not automatically calculated when a customer cancels.

6. Billing Errors and Duplicate Charges

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, charged more than once, or experienced another billing issue, please contact StudyForge with the relevant transaction information so the issue can be reviewed.

7. Refund Method

If a refund is approved, it will normally be returned through the original payment method used for the transaction, subject to the payment provider's processing rules.

8. Re-subscription

Cancelling a StudyForge Pro subscription does not permanently prevent a customer from subscribing again.

Customers may purchase a new subscription after cancellation, subject to the applicable pricing and subscription terms.

9. Payment Provider Rules

Payments and refunds may be processed through a third-party payment provider. Payment processing, settlement times and certain transaction requirements may therefore be subject to that provider's policies and applicable laws.

10. Contact

For billing questions or refund requests, please use the contact method provided by StudyForge on its website.

By subscribing to StudyForge Pro, customers acknowledge this Refund Policy together with the StudyForge Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
