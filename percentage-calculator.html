How to calculate a percentage

To find a percentage of a number, convert the percentage to a decimal and multiply it by the number.

Example: 15% of 240 Step 1: Convert 15% to a decimal: 15 ÷ 100 = 0.15 Step 2: Multiply by 240: 0.15 × 240 = 36 Answer: 15% of 240 is 36.

Percentage formulas

Percentage of a number: (percentage ÷ 100) × number What percentage is one number of another: (part ÷ whole) × 100 Percentage change: ((new value − original value) ÷ original value) × 100

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply the result by the number. For example, 20% of 50 is (20 ÷ 100) × 50 = 10.

How do I find what percentage one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 25 out of 200 is (25 ÷ 200) × 100 = 12.5%.

How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100. A positive result is an increase and a negative result is a decrease.
