Loan payment formula

For a fixed-rate amortizing loan, the monthly payment is calculated from the principal, monthly interest rate and number of payments.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1)
This is an estimate. Lender fees, taxes, insurance, variable rates and other terms can change the actual payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a longer loan reduce the monthly payment?

Usually yes, but a longer term can increase the total interest paid.

Does this include lender fees?

No. It estimates principal and interest only.
