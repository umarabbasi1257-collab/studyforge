How to use the currency converter

Converted amount = amount × fetched exchange rate

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the exchange rates live?

The calculator fetches the latest available reference rates from Frankfurter. These are updated on working days and should not be treated as real-time trading quotes.

Can I use any currency?

Yes. Type the currency codes or labels you want to display.
